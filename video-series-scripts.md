# Axion Deep Labs — Video Series Scripts & Recording Guide

**Format:** AI TTS voiceover + screen recording + Blender motion graphics + background music
**Platform:** All (YouTube long-form + cut for LinkedIn, X, TikTok/Reels)
**Products:** Site2CRM (site2crm.io) + Made4Founders (made4founders.com)
**Brand:** Axion Deep Labs (axiondeep.com)

---

## Production Notes

- **TTS Voice:** Use a natural, confident male/female voice (ElevenLabs or similar). Avoid robotic cadence. Script sentences are short for clean TTS delivery.
- **Music:** Lo-fi electronic or ambient tech. Low volume under voiceover, swell during transitions.
- **Blender:** Use for intro/outro logo animations, section title cards, stat callouts (floating numbers), and transition wipes between sections.
- **Pacing:** Hold each screen 3-4 seconds before cutting. Cursor moves slowly and deliberately.
- **Resolution:** 4K recording, export 1080p for YouTube, 1080x1920 vertical crops for Reels/TikTok.
- **Clips:** Each video should have 2-3 natural cut points for 30-60 second short-form clips.

>

>

>
> When enough contact info is captured — email plus name, or phone plus name — the lead is created in your CRM. A webhook fires. Your team gets notified.
>

You can store API keys, client secrets, bearer tokens, OAuth tokens, SSH private keys, encryption keys, Stripe publishable and secret keys, webhook secrets, PINs, security questions and answers, recovery codes, and backup codes. Each field is typed — text, secret, URL, date, or dropdown. Every single one is encrypted individually with its own random nonce.
>
> View your credentials in list mode or grid mode. Search by name or URL. Filter by category. Filter by business if you manage multiple companies. Sort by name, category, or date. Mark favorites for quick access — they show in a pinned section at the top.
>

Click copy to put the password on your clipboard — it calls the server, decrypts on the fly, and writes to your clipboard. The button turns green for two seconds to confirm.
