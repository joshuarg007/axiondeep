## Brand Positioning

AxionDeep is a **pure R&D holding company**. The website should NEVER contain:
- Sales language ("hire us," "our services," "get a quote," "pricing")
- Client-facing CTAs ("discuss a project," "start your project")
- Service offerings ("custom development," "enterprise solutions")
- Price ranges or SLAs

Instead, use:
- Research language ("investigate," "advance," "discover," "innovate")
- Lab/academic framing ("research collaborations," "technical inquiries," "partnerships")
- Product portfolio language ("built from research," "from the labs")
