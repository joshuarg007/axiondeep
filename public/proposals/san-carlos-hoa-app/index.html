01 / The Opportunity

A Complete Gate System, Built for Caracol Peninsula

Most LPR vendors quote a community an enterprise contract: tens of thousands for the hardware, a recurring platform fee, and a dashboard the board can use. Homeowners get nothing. We do the opposite. One package, under $3,000, gets you the cameras, the software, the mobile app for every homeowner, and the admin console for the board.

AI Cameras Included

Purpose-built AI cameras read every plate at the gate. On-device AI recognizes registered household vehicles and common service vehicles (police, trash, delivery) and logs everything else. No keypads, no QR codes for residents to remember.

Every Homeowner, Private Account

A single branded app on iOS and Android. Each homeowner logs into their own secure account. The board owns the community and controls who has access.

Built for San Carlos

Fully bilingual in English and Spanish, for a community of both expat and Mexican owners. Runs on the iPhones and Android phones residents already carry.

Pay as You Grow

Under $3,000 to set the whole system up. $1 per home per month after that. No flat service contract, no per-feature add-ons, no surprises.

What Every Homeowner Gets

A live feed of gate activity with vehicle photo, plate, make/model/color, time, and direction. Real-time push notifications. A "green list" for their household vehicles, so family cars show as recognized. Secure one-time codes to grant guests gate access, valid for a single visit or a date range. A separate Airbnb host mode for owners who rent short-term, so renters get gate access only during their stay. All private to their account, all on their phone.

What the HOA Gets

A web console to manage every resident: add, remove, suspend for unpaid dues, and assign to units. Broadcast community-wide alerts to every resident's phone. Service vehicles (police, trash, USPS, UPS, FedEx, delivery) auto-tagged in the feed. Flag and resolve suspicious vehicles. Pull activity reports any time. The board stays in control; the system does the work.

02 / What's Included

Everything in the Package

A single, complete build. No "Phase 2," no upgrades to buy later. The whole system, from the cameras at the gate to the app in every homeowner's pocket.

Hardware on Site

Component What's Included
AI LPR Cameras (entry + exit) Two purpose-built license-plate-recognition cameras mounted to capture every vehicle passing through your gate. Capture distance 5 to 40 ft, 4K resolution, color night vision, high plate-read accuracy.
Edge Processing Unit An on-site mini computer that runs the ALPR engine, classifies vehicles (resident, guest, service), and pushes events to the cloud in real time.
Gate Relay Interface Hardware module that opens the gate barrier when a recognized plate or valid guest code arrives. Connects to most existing gate operators.
On-Site Installation Camera mounting, cable runs, network configuration, gate operator integration, and a full operational test before handoff.

Software & App

Component What's Included
Resident App (iOS + Android) Live gate-activity feed with plate, vehicle photo, make/model/color, timestamp, and entry/exit direction. Searchable history. Real-time push notifications. Fully bilingual English/Spanish, branded for Caracol Peninsula.
Vehicle Green List Every homeowner registers their household's vehicles in the app. Recognized plates are auto-tagged on the feed so family cars do not look like strangers.
Service Vehicle Recognition The system auto-identifies common service vehicles (police, trash, USPS, UPS, FedEx, delivery) using make/model recognition, so residents and the board see at a glance what is at the gate.
Secure Visitor Codes Homeowners generate a secure one-time or date-bounded code from the app. The guest enters it at the gate intercom, or the homeowner sends the guest's plate ahead. Valid only during the window the homeowner sets.
Airbnb Host Mode Owners who rent short-term get a separate host role to issue stay-bounded gate access for renters. Codes expire automatically at check-out.
Flag & Report Residents flag a suspicious vehicle straight to HOA security from the feed. The board sees flagged vehicles and a resolution workflow.
HOA Admin Console Web console for the board: manage residents, suspend accounts for unpaid dues, assign units, broadcast community-wide alerts, pull activity reports, and review flagged vehicles.
Onboarding & Training Resident sign-up flow, board training call, and bilingual onboarding materials for the community rollout.

Assumptions & What's Not Included

Not included:

  • Internet service at the gate. If the community does not already have one, we will quote a cellular or fiber option separately.
  • The existing gate operator and barrier. We connect to what is already installed.
  • Hardware-based credentials (RFID cards, key fobs) require additional readers at the gate and are not part of the standard package. Secure visitor codes through the app handle the same use case without extra hardware.

This quote assumes:

  • One entry/exit gate at the community's main entrance.
  • Power available at the camera locations (low voltage, supplied through PoE).
  • The HOA designates one board contact and provides the resident roster.
  • San Carlos, Sonora install with reasonable site access.
