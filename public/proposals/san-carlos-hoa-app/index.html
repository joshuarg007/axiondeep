01 / The Opportunity

Every Homeowner, Their Own View of the Gate

Your community is installing the Inex Technologies IZCloud LPR Virtual Guard system. Its cameras automatically read and log every license plate entering and leaving through the gate. This proposal adds the one piece IZCloud deliberately leaves out: a private, cloud-based app that puts that gate activity in every homeowner's pocket, in real time.

IZCloud Has No Resident App

Inex's platform is built to be "app-free." Residents interact by voice and SMS, and only the HOA board gets a management dashboard. Individual homeowners currently have no way to see who is entering the community.

The Data Already Exists

Every plate, vehicle photo, make, model, color, and timestamp is already captured by the IZCloud cameras. We add no hardware. We surface data the HOA is already paying to collect.

One App, Private Accounts

A single app, branded for your community. Each homeowner signs into their own secure account on their own phone. The HOA owns the community account and controls who has access.

Built for San Carlos

Fully bilingual in English and Spanish, for a community of both expat and Mexican owners, running on the iPhones and Android phones residents already carry.

What Homeowners Get

A live feed of gate activity: license plate, vehicle photo, make/model/color, time, and whether it was an entry or an exit. Real-time push notifications the moment a vehicle passes the gate. A searchable history of everything that has come and gone. All private to their account, all on their phone.

What the HOA Gets

A simple web console to invite and manage homeowner accounts, assign units, and broadcast community-wide alerts (gate closures, security notices, board announcements) straight to every resident's phone. The board stays in control; the app does the work.
