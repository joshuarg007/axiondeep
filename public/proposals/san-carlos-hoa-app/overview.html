Smart Gate Access

The Caracol Peninsula Smart Gate

A simpler way to manage who comes through your community gate. No clickers, no fobs to lose.

A camera at the gate reads license plates. If you live here, the gate opens for you automatically. Everything else, your guests, your Airbnb visitors, your delivery drivers, is handled from a simple app on your phone.

How it works

For a resident, it is this simple:

1

You drive up

A camera at the gate reads your license plate, the same way a person would.

2

It recognizes you

The system checks that your plate belongs to a resident in good standing.

3

The gate opens

It opens in about a second. No clicker, no card, no app to fumble with.

What you get as a homeowner

A free app on your phone, in English or Spanish.

✓See gate activity live

Watch who is coming and going at the gate in real time, with a photo of each vehicle.

✓Get notified

A notification when your own car, or a family member's, arrives at the gate.

✓Let guests in easily

Send a guest a simple code to text them, or add their plate so the gate just opens for them.

✓Manage your vehicles

Add or remove the cars that should open the gate for you, any time.

✓Airbnb made easy

Renting your place out? Set up gate access for a guest's whole stay. It turns off automatically at checkout.

✓Report a problem

See a suspicious vehicle? Flag it from the app so the board and your neighbors know.

What the board gets

A simple dashboard to run the gate, no technical skills needed.

  • ●Manage residents. Add new homeowners, remove old ones, all in a few clicks.
  • ●Turn access on or off. For example, pause gate access for an account that is behind on dues, and restore it the moment they are caught up.
  • ●See everything. A full log of gate activity, with photos, and reports you can download.
  • ●Send community alerts. Push a message to every homeowner's phone at once.
  • ●Know your service vehicles. The system learns the trash, mail, and delivery trucks so you can tell them apart at a glance.

For your guests and visitors

A code they punch in

Text your guest a code. They enter it at the gate keypad and it lets them in. Codes can be one-time or good for a date range, and they expire on their own.

Or no code at all

Have them send you their license plate ahead of time. Add it in the app, and the gate simply opens when they arrive. Nothing to remember.

Built for the real world

Designed to keep working in an outage

The "brain" of the system sits right at the gate, not only in the cloud, so it is designed to keep letting residents in even if the internet goes down.

Built with privacy in mind

It reads license plates only, never faces, and it does not track people. Plate photos are automatically deleted after 30 days, and it is built to follow both US and Mexican privacy law.

The board stays in control

This is an automation tool, not a replacement for your board. The HOA decides who has access and sets the rules. The system simply carries them out.

Always a manual way in

The gate keeps its manual override and call box. In an emergency, or any time the system is down, there is always a way to open the gate.
