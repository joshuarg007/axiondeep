How it works

One platform, four moving parts.

📷1
Capture

Read the plate

Cameras at the gate read license plates day and night — on-camera (Axis) or via edge AI on the local unit.

⚙️2
Decide

Match & authorize

The edge unit checks the plate against the resident allow-list and visitor passes — even if the internet drops.

🔓3
Open

Trigger the gate

A trusted relay fires your existing gate operator. No proprietary gate hardware required.

📱4
Manage

Run it anywhere

The Axion app handles residents, visitor passes, logs, and alerts from any phone or browser.

Higher tiers run plate recognition on the camera itself — removing cloud-fallback cost and reducing the number of things that can fail.
