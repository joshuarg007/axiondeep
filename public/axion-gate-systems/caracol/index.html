What you get no matter which you choose

The price is what changes. These do not.

Setup & monthly

The one-time price already includes $500 setup. After that it is $30 a month, plus $1 per home. Prices are per gate; a gate with no wired internet adds a cellular router.

Our install work

We stand behind our installation. If something we installed was not done right, we come back and make it right.

App & support

The app is kept running for as long as you are with us. Support is during business hours; guaranteed response times are an optional add-on.

You stay in charge of your gate. It opens automatically for people you approve. A convenience tool, not a guard or alarm service.
You can always get in and out by hand. It does not control your exit or get in the way of your fire and ambulance override. Your manual gate release stays in place.
We work hard to keep it running. If a camera misses a plate or power or internet goes down, people still get in with a keypad code, guest pass, attendant, or manual override.
We are honest about the limits. No perfect plate reading, no promise that nothing unwanted ever gets in, no zero downtime. Anyone promising a "perfectly secure" gate is overselling.
Paying more buys better cameras, not a different promise. The higher options give you better accuracy, tougher equipment, trusted brands, and longer warranties.
